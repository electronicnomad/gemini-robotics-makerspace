================================================================================
BATTERY POWER SPECIFICATIONS FOR 10,000mAh SETUP (NO SOLAR)
================================================================================

1. RUNTIME CAPACITY ANALYSIS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
With your choice of a 10,000mAh battery pack and zero solar input, your total
deployment runtime depends entirely on the efficiency of your power-saving steps.

* Total Available Energy (Internal 3.7V Cells): 37.0 Watt-hours (Wh)
* Standard 5V Boost Converter Efficiency    : ~74% 
* Net Usable Output Capacity (at 5V)         : ~7,400 mAh

Runtime Estimates Based on System Configurations:

* OPTIMIZED CONFIGURATION (CSI Camera, USB Off, Audio/BT Off, Wi-Fi Power Save):
  - Current Draw: ~300 mA (1.5 Watts)
  - EXPECTED RUNTIME: ~24.6 Hours (Full Day of continuous operation)

* UNOPTIMIZED CONFIGURATION (Legacy USB Webcam, Full CPU Load, Active USB Bus):
  - Current Draw: ~1,200 mA (6.0 Watts)
  - EXPECTED RUNTIME: ~6.1 Hours


2. BATTERY CHOICE CRITERIA (WHAT TO LOOK FOR)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Since you are not using solar panels to constantly replenish power, ensure your 
chosen 10,000mAh power bank meets these critical technical requirements:

* Minimum Current Output: Must support at least 5V / 3.0A (15 Watts) on a single
  USB-C port. Lower output limits (like 5V / 2.1A) can trigger undervoltage 
  throttling during high-bandwidth Wi-Fi transmission bursts.
* Pass-Through Charging : Look for a power bank that supports charging while 
  discharging. This allows you to plug the bank into a wall outlet to charge 
  the battery without dropping the live Pi stream.
* Auto-On Feature       : Ensure the power bank automatically delivers power when 
  a device is connected, rather than requiring a physical button press, which 
  can complicate enclosed headless deployments.


3. DEPLOYMENT & MAINTENANCE CYCLE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Because this is a closed loop battery system, manage your swap cycle as follows:

* Daily Swap: Schedule a battery change or recharge window every 20-22 hours to
  maintain a safe overhead margin before the low-battery script flags an shutdown.
* Safe Swapping: Do not hot-swap the battery if it cuts power instantly. Thanks
  to the Read-Only Overlay Filesystem configured earlier, you can simply pull 
  the dead battery, plug in the fresh one, and the Pi will safely reboot back 
  into its streaming state with zero disk errors.
